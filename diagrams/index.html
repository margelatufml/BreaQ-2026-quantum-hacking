The idea that repeats

Every challenge hands you something that looks like evidence and asks whether it really proves the claim: a count that is correct but misread, a histogram that looks fair but is replayed, factors that could come from luck, an error rate that could be noise or an attacker. The scoring rewards finding the trust boundary, running one controlled experiment, and saying honestly what the evidence does not prove.
